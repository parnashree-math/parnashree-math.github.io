Hello, I am Parnashree Ghosh, an INSPIRE faculty fellow at the Department of Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore.
I am broadly interested in Algebraic geometry and Commutative Algebra. My current research interest lies in studying affine fibrations,  locally trivial affine bundles, embedding between $\mathbb{A}^1$-contractible algebraic varieties and Cremona equivalence classes, stably affine spaces and the related Cancellation Problem of varieties. 
I am also interested in teaching Mathematics at undergraduate and graduate levels.
