Hello, I am Parnashree Ghosh, an INSPIRE faculty fellow at the Department of Mathematics, Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore.
I am broadly interested in Algebraic geometry and Commutative Algebra. My current research interest lies in studying affine fibrations,  locally trivial affine bundles, Study of Cremona tranformations and Automorphism groups of varieties, embedding between $\mathbb{A}^1$-contractible algebraic varieties,  stably affine spaces and the related Cancellation Problem. 
I am also interested in teaching Mathematics at undergraduate and graduate levels.
